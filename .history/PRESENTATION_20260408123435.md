Each student is expected to prepare and present his or her own project professionally. Each presenter will be given a minimum of 5 minutes and a maximum of 8 minutes to deliver the presentation. Please manage your time well and make sure your explanation is clear, focused, and organized.
Your slides should not be text-heavy. Keep them compact, visually clear, and presentation-friendly. Focus on the most important points only. During your presentation, dial in to the problem your project addresses, briefly explain the methods you used, and provide a demonstration of the solution you developed.
You are also expected to wear smart casual attire during your presentation. Treat this as if you are presenting your project to potential investors. Be confident, professional, and prepared to clearly communicate the value, usefulness, and relevance of your project. The presentation will also be evaluated based on how useful and practical your project is, so be ready to explain why it matters and who can benefit from it.

Before you present, you are expected to print a copy of the scoring rubric that will be used in evaluating your presentation. The presentation deadline is on the day of your final examination. No extension will be given. You are encouraged to finish your presentation earlier than the final examination day.
